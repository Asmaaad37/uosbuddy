# Me

The 60 · 30 · 10 formula applied to Me (profile, settings and links).

## Audit (before)

- The profile card was a plain white card, the same as every list below it, so the screen had no
  focal point.
- It said "CS · Class of 2024"; the rest of the app says "Batch".
- List icons were grey; the formula wants Blue icons.
- Some subtitles restated the title or ran long ("Reporting, privacy, help", "Who sees your profile
  and can message you", "Notes and past papers from every course").

## Plan

1. **Profile card is the hero:** Midnight with white text, a white avatar with Midnight initials and
   a white verified badge (contrast passes in both themes, as on Explore).
2. **"CS · Batch 2024"**.
3. **Blue row icons** in the shared `Row` (every grouped list in the app picks this up) and the
   Appearance row.
4. **Shorter subtitles:** Safety centre loses its subtitle; Privacy → "Who sees you and can message
   you"; Resources → "Notes and past papers"; Admin → "Verification, reports, people".

Me is a hub with no single main action, so it has no orange button. "Finish your profile", the
settings lists, Log out and the footer are unchanged.
