# Chats

The 60 · 30 · 10 formula applied to the chat list (Chats, Requests and Groups tabs).

## Audit (before)

- No orange action: starting a chat is the screen's main action, but the compose button was plain.
- Unread was shown in Blue (row dot) and Midnight (tab bar count), so new messages didn't stand out
  from links and selected chips. The formula keeps orange for new and unread.
- Suggested group chats said "Class of 2024" (the app says "Batch" elsewhere) with grey wells.
- Empty-state lines ran to 13–16 words ("…Chats you start show up here.", "…it waits here until you
  accept.").

## Plan

1. **Compose (header) is the screen's one orange action** (Kinnow with a dark icon).
2. **Unread is Kinnow:** the row dot and the tab bar count. The count text and the row's
   "n unread" label carry the state, so it isn't shown by colour alone. Muted chats keep the grey dot.
3. **Empty states:** "Say hi to someone in your batch." and "Messages from people you're not
   connected with wait here." Their actions stay tertiary (Sky), so they don't compete with compose.
4. **Groups → Suggested:** "CS · Batch 2024" instead of "Class of" (the label shown only; the chat's
   own name is unchanged), and Sky wells with a Blue icon.

The segmented tabs, chat rows, the Groups "Suggested" list and the thread screen are unchanged here;
the thread and its sheets come with the remaining sub-screens.
